Most watchlists are dashboards showing what a stock is worth. Since is a diff: it shows what changed while you were away. That makes the baseline the whole engineering problem. The baseline is personal, held as a monotonic server-side watermark so two devices can't corrupt it. It decays, so corporate actions apply as adjustment factors keyed by ISIN — a 1:10 split is not a 90% crash, and Since won't say it is. It expires, so liveness is judged per symbol across four states: live, closed, halted, degraded. Silence is the default, because a watchlist that shouts trains overtrading.
